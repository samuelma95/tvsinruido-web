Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how the TV Sin Ruido mobile app works. Its controller is Samuel Mateo Andrade, trading as DStudio Labs. For privacy questions or support, contact info@donmarketing.es.

Data used by the app

After you enable debugging and approve pairing on the TV, the app connects directly from your phone to a compatible Android TV or Google TV on your local network using ADB. To show a diagnosis and enable restoration, it may process the TV model, system version, installed packages and their status, actions performed, and a local copy of the previous state. The network address is used to establish the connection. The ADB connection key is stored in the phone's secure storage.

This technical data and the restoration snapshots stay on your phone. They are not sent automatically to TV Sin Ruido servers. The app requires no account and has no ads or automatic analytics.

Optional error reports and contact

We receive information only if you choose to email us. Before opening your email app, TV Sin Ruido previews a report prepared locally. You can edit it or cancel. The automatic report includes the app version, platform, failed step, and a generic error code. It does not include the TV model or firmware, ADB key, pairing code, network address, or serial number. Please review your message before sending it, as any text you add may contain other personal data.

If you contact support, we process your email address, message, and any technical details you choose to provide to answer your request. The legal basis is providing the support you request and, where applicable, meeting legal obligations. Your email app and provider will also process the message under their own policies.

Retention, deletion, and recipients

You can delete TV Sin Ruido's local data in your phone's system settings or by uninstalling the app. Restore TV changes first if you want to keep that option: deleting app data also deletes restoration snapshots saved on the phone. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; you may request deletion where applicable.

We do not sell data or share it with advertisers. Support messages are processed by the email service used to receive them. We do not obtain or store Google Play payment details; Google handles purchases under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding data we receive by email, and other applicable rights, at info@donmarketing.es. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

This page

This policy is hosted as a static GitHub Pages site. The page sets no cookies and uses no tracking tools of its own. GitHub may process technical access data when serving it; see its privacy statement.

The app is intended for people who can configure TV debugging. It is not designed for children.
